Curve inverse — fit a circle

Enter 3 or more point numbers (in order along the arc, comma-separated). 3 points use the closed-form perpendicular-bisector method; 4+ points use algebraic least-squares. Per-point residuals are shown so you can spot field-measured outliers.

DXF export → Carlson / AutoCAD

Exports a DXF R12 ASCII file. Carlson Survey, Civil3D, and BricsCAD all read this directly. Coordinates are in US Survey Feet, X = Easting, Y = Northing.

Rotate Traverse

Rotates every bearing call in the active traverse. Use By angle to rotate a known amount, or By bearing pair to reconcile a measured bearing with the correct one (common for GLO retracements).

Positive = clockwise. Negative = counter-clockwise. Value in decimal degrees.
Enter both bearings to compute rotation delta.

Join Traverses

Appends all non-POB rows from the source traverse to the end of the active traverse. The source traverse is not modified. Useful for combining two deed descriptions that share a common point.

Edit compound curve

Two circular arcs sharing a tangent at the PCC. Both arcs must turn the same direction. Give any two of {Δ, R, L} for each arc; the third is solved. The chord bearing in the row's bearing column is the chord of arc 1 (PC → PCC). Δ entry is d.mmss packed — same as the bearing field (e.g. 3.2012 = 3°20'12").
